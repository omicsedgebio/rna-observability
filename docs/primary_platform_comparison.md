# Structure-blind primary platform comparison decision

The preferred endogenous pair is K562 Illumina short-read and ONT direct RNA, both quantified with the SG-NEx Ensembl 91 Salmon outputs. This choice was made from methods and measurement-only QC, without icSHAPE values. It holds quantifier family and reference approximately fixed, but Salmon modes differ: short-read bias and effective-length correction versus long-read alignment and ONT mode. The comparison is between **relative abundance measurements**, not between truth and error.

The proposed representation is `normEst` interpreted as method TPM, reclosed to 1,000,000 across exactly the common authoritative transcript index in every library. Keep original TPM as normalization sensitivity. Fixed annotation and full per-library transcript coverage must be checked before any formal contrast. Illumina K562 Rep3 is the only currently independent abundance covariate library if Rep4/5 define the endpoint. The four ONT direct-RNA single-run preparations are replicate1, replicate4, replicate5 and replicate6. Do not match biological preparations from numeric labels across laboratories. Aggregate technical runs within a verified preparation with depth weights, then take protocol medians across independent preparations. The primary sample unit is biological preparation; transcript rows are repeated measurements and genes are dependent units.

Quantifier sensitivity: RSEM versus Salmon for Illumina; NanoCount and Bambu versus Salmon for ONT, with the latter two restricted to detection/rank or count-scale sensitivity because they lack commensurate continuous abundance semantics. Annotation sensitivity: Salmon on the alternate bambu annotation only after transcript remapping, not a direct substitute. Protocol sensitivity may examine ONT cDNA separately, never pool it into direct RNA.

This remains a **candidate**, not a frozen platform estimand. The structure mapping has zero validated A/B transcripts; the independent abundance covariate has only one library; 56.8% of the independently expression-eligible transcripts are all-zero in direct RNA; offset choice shifts the median signed contrast substantially. These failures prevent outcome freeze and Models A/B/C.
