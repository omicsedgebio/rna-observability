# Phase 3C detection endpoint selection

Detection candidates were evaluated without structure information: quantifier-specific calls, replicate-supported calls, cross-quantifier consensus, and sparse-output-aware calls. The threshold grid was 0.1, 1 and 5 TPM with Illumina support rules 2/2 and direct-RNA support rules 2/4, 3/4 and 4/4. At 1 TPM, Illumina replicate call agreement was 0.869, mean direct-RNA replicate agreement was 0.880, and disjoint direct-RNA pair agreement was 0.920. The previously described 2/2 Illumina and 3/4 direct-RNA rule gives BOTH 11,731, ILLUMINA_ONLY 20,201, DIRECT_RNA_ONLY 315, NEITHER 3,687 and INDETERMINATE 13,375 in the independently expressed universe; these counts are measurement summaries, not absence or truth.

The rule is a useful candidate because intermediate support is retained as indeterminate and zeros are not replaced by a pseudocount. It is not yet final. Salmon versus RSEM status agreement is 0.861 on their complete Illumina rows, whereas direct-RNA Salmon versus NanoCount agreement is 0.519 on the much smaller complete NanoCount subset. NanoCount absent rows are not zeros, and a consensus requiring NanoCount would discard most transcripts. A consensus of Salmon and Bambu is also not an independent molecular truth because both workflows share annotation and their positive-assignment agreement is zero-inflated.

The 1 TPM, 2/2 versus 3/4 rule is therefore recorded as the leading candidate, with 0.1 and 5 TPM and alternative support rules as sensitivity analyses. Selection is `PENDING_FINAL_REVIEW`; no final primary endpoint or frozen plan is declared in Phase 3C interim work.
