# Leakage-resistant held-out design, pending freeze

Status: prespecified design, **not frozen** because the matched cohort is empty. Each transcript belongs to its gene group. Exact duplicate transcript sequences occur across genes (398 sequence hashes, 970 transcripts, up to 16 genes per hash) and show that gene-only splitting can leak molecular identity. The future group is a connected component of gene membership plus cross-gene transcript sequence similarity. At minimum connect genes sharing an exact transcript sequence; before freeze, compute cross-gene near-similarity using the fixed Ensembl 91 FASTA, same-strand sequence identity at least 90% over at least 80% of the shorter transcript, and shared exon loci where coordinates support it. Inspect component sizes without outcomes; if one giant component makes five folds impossible, document an outcome-blind threshold revision before freeze. Paralog families should remain in one component when they meet the prespecified similarity criterion.

Tentative outer validation: five balanced grouped folds, three deterministic repeats with seeds 230923, 230924 and 230925. Four inner grouped folds on each outer training split tune a fixed penalty grid. Training-only preprocessing, imputation and normalization parameters cannot use held-out rows. The exact same outer rows and folds compare A, B, C and future D. Groups are assigned with no access to endpoint or structure scores. Metrics are equal-gene-weighted MAE and RMSE for continuous endpoints, with group-bootstrap uncertainty over held-out gene/similarity components; library/preparation uncertainty is assessed separately because gene resampling does not create biological replicates. Calibration intercept/slope and abundance-stratified errors are secondary. Report fold-wise and pooled out-of-fold metrics, avoiding a t-test on correlated folds.

Technical sequencing runs within one RNA preparation are first aggregated, never split into independent biological samples. K562 Illumina and direct-RNA preparations are not assumed paired by replicate number. Across independent studies, same transcript sequence appearing in training and external test is labeled transfer to a new context, not novel-transcript generalization. The exact group graph, fold assignments and hyperparameter grid remain uncommitted requirements for a future freeze. `src/rnaobs/core.py` implements deterministic connected components and grouped fold assignment, with synthetic leakage tests.
