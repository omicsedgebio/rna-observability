# Phase 3C spike-in endpoint calibration

SG-NEx provides synthetic spike-in truth through the processed `spikein.rds` object. The extracted truth table contains 635 molecules across Sequin, SIRV and ERCC groups. The calibration uses the four processed mixture families and compares known positive concentration fractions with each workflow's reported `estimates` and `normEst`. It reports observed-row fraction, positive fraction, rank correlation and median absolute log10 relative error. Missing sparse-output rows remain missing and are never recoded as zero.

Across the Illumina mixtures, median `normEst` rank correlations were 0.7448 for RSEM and 0.7363 for Salmon. Across direct-RNA mixtures they were 0.5742 for NanoCount, 0.5477 for Bambu and 0.5025 for Salmon. Median observed fractions were approximately 0.99 for the explicit-output Illumina and Bambu workflows, 0.985 for direct-RNA Salmon, and 0.193 for direct-RNA NanoCount. These values calibrate behavior in synthetic mixtures only; they do not establish endogenous molecular truth or a common platform scale.

The full lightweight result is `results/tables/spikein_endpoint_calibration.tsv`, generated by `scripts/phase3c_spikein_calibration.py`. It is a separate true-error calibration endpoint and cannot be substituted for endogenous platform disagreement.
