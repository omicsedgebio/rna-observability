No directional claim for global structure effect: RT, ligation, fragmentation, accessibility and selection can act differently. More ambiguity is expected to worsen quantification difficulty under the baseline, but empirical disagreements need not increase monotonically.

A precise null with narrow uncertainty is informative. An imprecise null is inconclusive. If H3 fails under adequate coverage, power and controls, reject the central incremental-structure hypothesis for the specified scope; do not pivot endpoints to preserve it.
