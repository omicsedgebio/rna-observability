# Phase 3C quantifier endpoint audit

The audit reads only the SG-NEx processed K562 object and the Ensembl 91 annotation. It evaluates row availability, positive assignment, rank concordance and threshold-call agreement within biological preparations and across methods. It does not read structure scores or LongBench outcomes.

The processed object contains three Illumina Salmon runs, three Illumina RSEM runs, four direct-RNA Salmon runs, four direct-RNA NanoCount runs and four direct-RNA Bambu runs. NanoCount represents only a sparse subset of transcripts (about 30,553 transcripts present in every direct-RNA run in the row-availability audit), so its missing rows cannot be interpreted as measured zeros. Bambu `normEst` is CPM-like in this object; it is not placed on the TPM threshold grid. Cross-method quantitative comparisons involving Bambu are rank/positive-assignment diagnostics only.

Median within-method replicate Spearman correlations were 0.811 for Illumina Salmon, 0.801 for Illumina RSEM, 0.677 for direct-RNA Salmon, 0.837 for direct-RNA NanoCount on its represented rows, and 0.712 for direct-RNA Bambu. The conditional nature of the NanoCount number makes it unsuitable as a general detection reference. Same-run cross-method median rank correlations were 0.797 for Illumina Salmon versus RSEM, 0.497 for direct-RNA Salmon versus NanoCount, 0.668 for direct-RNA Salmon versus Bambu, and 0.476 for NanoCount versus Bambu. The corresponding positive-assignment agreement for Salmon versus NanoCount was 0.394 and for Salmon versus Bambu was 0.924; the latter is dominated by shared zeros and does not establish quantitative equivalence.

The complete rows are in `results/tables/quantifier_concordance.tsv`; run-level availability is in `results/tables/quantifier_run_inventory.tsv`. Thresholds of 0.1, 1 and 5 are reported only for workflows with TPM-like `normEst`. The audit supports retaining separate detection and rank/quantitative phenotype candidates. It does not support claiming an algorithm-independent ONT abundance scale, so the primary endpoint remains pending final review.
