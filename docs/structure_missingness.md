# icSHAPE availability and selection

This analysis uses only icSHAPE transcript IDs, reported lengths, callable-base counts and callable fractions. It does **not** read reactivity values, outcome values or model residuals. Because source icSHAPE exon and sequence definitions remain unverified, its usable flag is provisional: exact versioned ID plus matching transcript length, at least 50 callable bases and callable fraction at least 0.5. It is a QC proxy, not an A/B matched analysis universe.

There are 35,309 provisional usable profiles among 199,216 sequence-validated Ensembl 91 transcripts (17.72%). Threshold counts are 48,048 at fraction 0.25, 35,309 at 0.5 and 20,941 at 0.75, each also requiring 50 callable bases. The adjusted QC includes 198,569 transcripts with complete Salmon K562 abundance/detection covariates and 56,853 gene clusters; 51,629 of those genes have no usable profile. Usability rises from 6.49% at zero Illumina TPM to 38.91% at 1-10 TPM and 89.09% above 100 TPM. Length, GC, isoform count, exon sharing and short/long-read detectability were included in a descriptive multivariable spline model. The initial unpenalized clustered binomial GLM was rank-deficient and did not converge; the recorded alternative is standardized L2 logistic regression with `C=1`. Its coefficients are descriptive and have no inferential confidence intervals. Full coefficients, fixed-bin summaries and QC figures are generated under `results/tables/` and `results/figures/qc/`.

Overlap is poor: 47.37% of transcripts have fitted availability below 0.05, and naive inverse-probability weights among available profiles have a maximum of 12,608 and an effective sample size of approximately 193. A population-wide inverse probability weighted structure increment would be unstable and also relies on an untestable missing-at-random assumption. Complete-case analysis changes the target population and must not be generalized to all transcripts. A future analysis would need a prespecified high-overlap abundance/coverage stratum, availability-matched comparison or explicitly conditional complete-case estimand, with gene-level uncertainty and sensitivity to unmeasured availability factors. None can be frozen until source transcript mappings and an eligible cohort exist. Current missingness is too severe for unrestricted population inference.

The structure assay and SG-NEx sequencing originate from different laboratories/preparations, so abundance-dependence of availability is descriptive, not evidence that a per-transcript reactivity value predicts platform behavior.
