# Data reuse and licensing

The SG-NEx registry and repository identify the processed resource as CC BY-NC 4.0. This supports academic and publication-oriented analysis with attribution and non-commercial conditions, subject to the exact release terms. The future commercial status of OmicsEdgeBio is not resolved by this research repository.

GEO provides access to GSE145805 and its supplementary files, but the GEO record does not by itself grant a blanket redistribution or commercial license for every underlying source. The specific publication and repository terms must be retained with the provenance record. LongBench metadata inspected during Phase 1 identifies a CC BY 4.0 catalog license, but this does not remove the external-validation lock or authorize unreviewed redistribution of every component.

Derived tables, feature matrices, model parameters, and website hosting may create separate attribution, database-rights, or non-commercial questions. This document makes no legal conclusion. Academic analysis and a paper are currently feasible subject to attribution and source terms. A commercial explorer requires a separate legal review and may require alternative permissively licensed resources, such as appropriately licensed ENCODE or benchmark data.
