## Additional falsification checks

- Historical C remains a provenance benchmark and is not allowed to serve as the primary comparator merely because it would produce a larger apparent structure increment.
- Mean reactivity and thresholded high/low fractions are excluded rather than tried and selected. This prevents a feature-representation search but means the result pertains only to median enrichment.
- Original miniQuant K-value is read-model dependent and gene-level. Its inclusion strengthens the comparator but does not make the baseline quantifier-complete or platform-neutral.
- The enhanced annotation matrix is not currently present. During `PRESTRUCTURE_IMPLEMENTATION_LOCK` it must be materialized from pinned non-structure resources as exactly 15,999 rows in frozen order, with every locked annotation control, no outcome- or structure-derived feature, and its values and SHA256 fixed before structure access. Raw missing values for the two fold-locally imputed variables may remain; global imputation is prohibited.
- The strict no-imputation policy for structure protects the estimand but can yield INCONCLUSIVE if the precomputed callability bridge and eventual value parser disagree. That is preferable to a post-unblinding cohort repair.
- The fixed class-instability rule can downgrade `SUPPORTED` to `PARTIALLY_SUPPORTED` when every partial-support gate passes. It is descriptive, must be prominently named as a deficiency in partial-support language, and cannot upgrade `NOT_SUPPORTED`.
- A successful Mac execution would not complete canonical Linux reproduction. A Linux discrepancy cannot be resolved by selecting the more favorable result.

## Revision-specific confirmation

- `PARTIALLY_SUPPORTED` can no longer be produced by a negligible effect alone, even when the paired bootstrap lower bound exceeds zero.
- Failure of negative-control calibration prevents both `SUPPORTED` and `PARTIALLY_SUPPORTED`; every valid such result is `NOT_SUPPORTED`.
- A nonpositive stricter-callability sensitivity delta prevents both `SUPPORTED` and `PARTIALLY_SUPPORTED`; every valid such result is `NOT_SUPPORTED`.
- Partial support requires at least three of five fold-specific macro-F1 deltas to be strictly positive. Full support continues to require at least four of five.
- The implementation lock removes discretion between scientific design and structure unblinding by requiring the exact implementation, guards, tests, and hashes before access.
- Annotation-matrix values and exact ordering must be fixed and hashed before structure access; fold-local imputations are deliberately not materialized globally.
- Real GSE132099 structure values remain unread. Structure features computed: zero. Model D fits: zero. C_ANNOTATION fits: zero. C_QUALITY fits: zero. LongBench inputs read: zero.
