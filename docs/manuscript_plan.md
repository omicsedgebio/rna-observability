# Provisional manuscript plan

Working title: Molecular correlates of technology-specific RNA measurement behavior. No authorship or conclusion finalized.

1. Introduction: established bias/identifiability literature; specific unresolved incremental question.
2. Definition of transcript measurement behavior: estimand, independent unit, disagreement versus truth.
3. Cross-platform heterogeneity: replicate stability, depth/protocol/quantifier sensitivity.
4. Molecular determinants: interpretable A/B/C comparisons and identifiability controls.
5. Incremental role of experimental RNA structure: D>C, coverage controls, uncertainty, nulls.
6. Predictive framework: grouped evaluation, effects, calibration and explicit intended scope.
7. Independent external validation: locked evaluation; structural and nonstructural validation distinguished.
8. Limitations: observational inference, extracted versus living RNA, cross-study mismatch, missingness, old/new chemistry and quantifier dependence.
9. Data/code availability: rights, provenance, versions, public release only after approval.

## Figure and table reservations (not results)

| Figure | Role | Evidence needed |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Study design/datasets/platforms | Verified provenance and flow diagram |
| 2 | Transcript-specific disagreement | Stable phenotype and replicate uncertainty |
| 3 | Molecular associations | Adjusted effect estimates and controls |
| 4 | Experimental structure increment | Held-out D>C with matched cohort/nulls |
| 5 | Protocol-specific prediction/calibration | Out-of-fold estimates with uncertainty |
| 6 | Locked external validation | Frozen predictions scored once; include failure |

Supplement: negative controls, missingness, mapping failures, sensitivity to abundance thresholds/offsets, replicate robustness, protocol versions and any exploratory localization.

Tables: source/protocol inventory; exact inclusion flow; feature definitions; model comparisons; external validation scope. All output generated by scripts from versioned inputs/configuration. No hand-edited data figures. Empty manuscript directories are intentional.

A paper is supported by meaningful reproducible incremental information OR a precise, well-controlled null resolving an existing mechanistic expectation. It is weakened by small unstable gains, missingness-driven association or same-gene-only transfer. Lack of phenotype reproducibility, irreparable provenance/coordinate mismatch, or adequate-precision nulls across independent contexts defeats the central structural claim. Do not write conclusions before evidence.
