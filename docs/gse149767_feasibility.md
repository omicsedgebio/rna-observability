# GSE149767 independent K562 structure feasibility

[GEO GSE149767](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE149767), from the Yeo laboratory, includes K562 in-vivo icSHAPE and fSHAPE alongside other cell contexts. [Sample processing metadata](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSM4795047) states hg38 for icSHAPE/fSHAPE; it does not pin an Ensembl or RefSeq release. The processed in-vivo archive contains 10,896 `.rx` transcript profiles with two replicate reactivity columns and `-999.0` meaning uncalled. Filenames carry chromosome/strand and RefSeq NM/NR accession, not Ensembl ID. The readme describes fSHAPE `.map` files with position, reactivity, variance and base. It does not provide an independent in-vivo FASTA. These assays are distinct; fSHAPE values were **not** substituted for in-vivo icSHAPE values.

The minimum processed materials needed for a sequence compatibility check were downloaded: in-vivo `.rx` archive, 89,111,498 bytes, SHA256 `e21c6f16adf4e79c24dc75100131b5585373465425e0391c06f7fa9c3ad52ecf`; readme, 3,551 bytes, SHA256 `cb62bb14c125831d14ea8b6d9b3828686fc565aee43cf68837b7c7a6f4aeb0a4`; K562 fSHAPE `.map` archive, 322,087,573 bytes, SHA256 `8f552225bfba88ef69b9f2309078c820bcb1b2a22be103226039868cb99f4df3`. Exact GEO URLs and UTC retrieval dates are in `metadata/phase3b_resources.tsv`. All files remain in ignored cache. No raw sequencing files were downloaded.

For each in-vivo filename, `scripts/phase3b_gse149767.py` seeks a unique same-RefSeq fSHAPE map, checks its full base sequence and length, then compares its sequence SHA256 against the validated Ensembl 91 FASTA. Counts over 10,896 in-vivo profiles: 10,149 lack an exact Ensembl 91 sequence, 724 have a unique exact match, 18 lack a unique sequence map, 4 map/in-vivo lengths disagree and 1 has multiple Ensembl 91 sequence matches. Of the 724, 353 overlap coverage-eligible GSE132099 A transcripts. Only 75 pass at least 50 callable bases and at least 0.5 fraction in both GSE149767 in-vivo replicate columns. Matching a same-accession fSHAPE sequence is a proxy for in-vivo transcript sequence identity, not a directly pinned in-vivo annotation.

Classification: **WEAK** for independent validation of transcript-level structure summaries, because the overlap is small, annotation provenance is incomplete and cross-study median summaries have low, uncertain concordance. It is **UNSUITABLE** for validating incremental structure contribution to a sequencing outcome: this dataset has no matched Illumina/ONT transcript phenotype. The structure-to-structure result is in `docs/structure_reproducibility_plan.md` and `results/tables/structure_reproducibility_summary.json`. GEO public access is established; dataset-specific redistribution/commercial terms remain unclear.
