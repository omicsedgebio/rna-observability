# GSE132099 availability and missing-data strategy

Availability is selective. Across 198,539 sequence-feature and independent-abundance-complete transcripts, 15,999 (8.06%) have A-mapped GSE132099 profiles meeting at least 50 callable bases and 0.5 callable fraction. Among 49,309 transcripts with independent Illumina Rep3 TPM at least 1, 11,687 are usable. A descriptive abundance/length/GC stratum (TPM at least 1, length 500-5,000 nt, GC 0.3-0.7) has 10,281 usable among 41,165, or 25.0%. This stratum was chosen as a diagnostic before any structure-outcome analysis, not as a fitted success criterion.

The availability-only ridge logistic diagnostic includes log independent abundance, log transcript length, GC, log isoforms per gene, exon count and unique 31-mer fraction. Its fitted availability probability is below 0.05 for 71.1% of the covariate-complete universe; 2.4% are below 0.01. Naive inverse probability weighting reaches weight 246.6 and effective sample size 6,302 among 15,999 available profiles. These are in-sample descriptive diagnostics, not calibrated propensity estimates. Full-population IPW is rejected because most transcript strata have poor overlap, missingness may depend on unmeasured assay factors, and weighting cannot recover structure values that were never measured.

Use an explicit **complete-case conditional estimand** as defined in `docs/structure_estimand.md`, with gene/sequence group uncertainty. Report attrition by abundance, length, GC, isoform count and sequence uniqueness using `results/tables/gse132099_missingness_strata.tsv`. Compare the prespecified 0.25/0.5/0.75 callable-fraction thresholds and high-overlap expression strata as sensitivity analyses, without tuning them on a structure association. Do not claim population-wide effects. Matching may be used only as a sensitivity within common support, never to manufacture missing structure measurements. Structure features must include callable fraction as a nuisance/control comparison so observed signal is not merely coverage.

The script `scripts/phase3b_missingness.py` reads validated IDs, callable masks/counts, annotation/sequence features and the held-apart abundance source. It does not read a reactivity score, platform disagreement endpoint or LongBench result. The per-stratum and model summaries are deterministic and reproducible from cached inputs.
